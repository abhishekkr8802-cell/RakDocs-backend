About Bank Statement to Excel Converter

Bank statements from your net banking portal come as PDF files that are difficult to work with — you cannot filter, sort, or analyse transactions directly. RakDocs converts your bank statement PDF into a fully editable Excel spreadsheet in seconds.

The converter automatically extracts every transaction row — including date, transaction narration/description, debit amount, credit amount, and closing balance — and places each into its own column. The result is a clean XLSX file you can open in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet software.

This tool works with digital bank statement PDFs (downloaded from net banking) as well as scanned paper statements. For the best accuracy, always use the digital PDF exported directly from your bank's app or website rather than a photographed or scanned copy.

RakDocs uses a dedicated backend server to handle all conversions. Your bank statement is processed securely and deleted immediately after you download the result — we never store, share, or access your financial documents.
